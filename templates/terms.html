1. Membership & Eligibility

Membership at The Royal Gym is open to individuals aged 18 years and above. Applicants under 18 may join only with the written consent of a parent or legal guardian, who must co-sign the membership form.

Each member must present a valid government-issued photo ID at the time of registration. Membership cards are personal and non-transferable. Allowing another person to use your card may result in immediate termination of your membership without refund.

The gym reserves the right to refuse, suspend, or terminate any membership at its sole discretion when a member violates these terms.

2. Health & Fitness Disclaimer

Members are strongly advised to consult a qualified physician before starting any new exercise program, especially if they have a pre-existing medical condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from an injury or surgery.

The Royal Gym is a fitness facility and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Trainers and staff are not licensed medical professionals. Any guidance provided is general in nature and should not be treated as medical recommendation.

Members participate in all activities at their own risk and accept full responsibility for any health-related outcomes of their training.

3. Code of Conduct

Members are expected to treat staff, trainers, and fellow members with courtesy and respect at all times. Harassment, discrimination, threatening behavior, intoxication, or use of foul language will not be tolerated and may lead to immediate suspension.

Appropriate athletic attire and clean indoor footwear must be worn in all training areas. Shirts must be worn at all times outside of designated changing facilities.

Mobile phones should be kept on silent mode. Photography and video recording inside the gym are not permitted without prior written consent from management.

4. Equipment Use & Safety

Members must use all equipment in accordance with posted instructions and trainer guidance. If you are unsure how to operate a machine, please ask a staff member before using it.

Re-rack weights and return all accessories (dumbbells, bars, mats, clips) to their designated location after use. Wipe down equipment with the provided cleaning supplies after every set or session.

Damage to equipment caused by misuse, negligence, or willful action may be charged to the responsible member at replacement cost. Any malfunction must be reported to the front desk immediately.

5. Personal Trainers & Group Classes

Personal training sessions and group classes must be booked in advance through the front desk or member portal. Bookings are confirmed on a first-come, first-served basis subject to trainer and class availability.

Cancellations must be made at least 12 hours before the scheduled session. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or no-shows, will be deducted from the member's session balance and are non-refundable.

Only certified Royal Gym trainers may provide paid personal training on the premises. Members are not permitted to conduct paid coaching sessions for other members or guests inside the facility.

6. Hours of Operation & Access

The gym is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, accessible to members via their registered membership card. The Royal Gym reserves the right to close the facility or restrict access for maintenance, public holidays, or in any emergency.

Notice of planned closures will be posted at the front desk and on the member portal at least 48 hours in advance whenever possible.

7. Cancellation, Refund & Freeze Policy

Members may cancel their membership at any time by providing written notice at least 30 days before the next billing date. Notice received after the billing date will take effect from the following billing cycle.

Membership fees are non-refundable for partial months. Pre-paid annual or quarterly memberships may be eligible for a pro-rated refund only in cases of documented relocation, long-term medical incapacity, or military deployment, subject to management approval.

Memberships may be temporarily frozen for up to 90 days per calendar year for medical reasons or extended travel, with prior written request and supporting documentation where applicable.

8. Personal Property & Lockers

Day-use lockers are provided as a courtesy. Members must bring their own lock and remove all belongings before leaving the facility. Locks left overnight will be removed and the locker emptied.

The Royal Gym is not responsible for loss, theft, or damage to any personal property brought onto the premises, including items stored in lockers, parking areas, or training floors.

9. Privacy & Data Use

The Royal Gym collects personal information (name, email, mobile number, billing details, health declarations) solely for the purpose of providing and managing your membership. We do not sell this information to third parties.

Member data is retained for the duration of the membership and for up to 24 months after cancellation, after which it is deleted or anonymized. A separate Privacy Policy detailing data handling practices will be published on this site.

By signing up, members consent to the collection and use of their data as described above.

10. Liability Waiver

Members acknowledge that physical exercise carries inherent risks, including but not limited to muscle strain, joint injury, and cardiac events. By using the facility, members voluntarily assume all such risks.

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, members release The Royal Gym, its owners, employees, trainers, and contractors from any liability for injury, illness, loss, or damage arising out of the use of the facility, except where caused by gross negligence or willful misconduct of the gym.

The Royal Gym reserves the right to modify these terms at any time. Continued use of the facility after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
